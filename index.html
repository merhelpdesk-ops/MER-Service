MER Decentralized Exchange Feature Introduction (Swap & Contracts) ▲
Platform Features Overview:

1. MER Decentralized Exchange Swap is a trading platform that allows you to swap cryptocurrencies across supported blockchain networks with customizable slippage settings and standard platform service fees.

2. MER Decentralized Exchange Contracts allows you to trade perpetual futures using cryptocurrency with customizable leverage multipliers. Risk Warning: Crypto futures trading carries significantly higher risk than spot swapping. The risk is determined by your leverage ratio. Higher leverage increases trading risk and amplifies both profits and losses, directly impacting position safety. Please choose responsibly based on your needs.

3. Funding Rates in MER Decentralized Exchange Contracts:
- Anchoring Spot Price: Encourages shorting/closing longs when futures trade at a premium, and longing/closing shorts when futures trade at a discount.
- Balancing Long/Short Positions: Adjusts market position ratios through financial incentives (interest compensation).
- Maintaining Market Health: Prevents price manipulation and excessive leverage accumulation in one-sided markets.
- Funding Rate Payments: When positive (+), long positions pay short positions. When negative (-), short positions pay long positions. Settlements occur every 8 hours.

4. Liquidation in MER Decentralized Exchange Contracts: When your margin is insufficient to support your perpetual futures position, MER Decentralized Exchange Contracts will execute a forced closure (liquidation).

5. MER Risk Disclaimer: Crypto and derivatives trading carry extremely high risk. MER platform assumes no compensation liability or obligation. Before participating in trading, please carefully evaluate your risk tolerance or consult professional advisors. MER solely provides trading features and platform services.
