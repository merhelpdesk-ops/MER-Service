MER DEX Perps System Rules & Trading Guide ▲
I. Pricing Mechanism
The platform uses a 3-price mechanism to ensure fairness, stability, and protection against market manipulation:
• Last Price: The most recent trade execution price on the order book. Used solely for charts, depth, and limit order matching.
• Index Price: The volume-weighted average price aggregated from major spot exchanges (e.g., Binance, OKX, Coinbase).
• Mark Price: The primary reference price used to calculate unrealized PnL (uPnL) and trigger liquidations.

II. Margin & Leverage Modes
• Cross Margin: All positions share a single margin pool.
• Isolated Margin: Risk is independently isolated to individual positions.
• Tiered Margin: Dynamic maintenance margin requirements that scale proportionally with larger position sizes.

III. Funding Rates
Periodic settlements to keep perpetual contract prices aligned with spot index prices.

IV. Take Profit / Stop Loss
Conditional orders triggered by the mark price or last price.

V. Liquidation, Bankruptcy & Auto-Deleveraging (ADL)
A multi-tiered liquidation engine utilizing partial liquidation, insurance funds, and auto-deleveraging.

Risk Disclaimer
Leveraged financial trading carries significant financial risk. By using MER DEX Perps, you acknowledge and agree to these terms.
