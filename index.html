MER DEX Perpetuals (Perps) System Rules & Trading Guide ▲
I. Price Mechanism
The platform uses a triple-price mechanism to ensure fairness, stability, and protection against market manipulation:
• Last Price: The most recent matched trade price on the order book. Used only for charts, depth, and limit order matching.
• Index Price: Weighted average price aggregated from major spot exchanges (e.g., Binance, OKX, Coinbase).
• Mark Price: Primary reference price used for calculating unrealized PnL (uPnL) and triggering liquidations.

II. Margin & Leverage Modes
• Cross Margin: Shared margin pool across all positions.
• Isolated Margin: Risk isolated per individual position.
• Tiered Margin (MMR): Dynamic maintenance margin requirements scale with larger position sizes.

III. Funding Rates
Settled periodically to align perpetual contract prices with spot index prices.

IV. Take Profit / Stop Loss (TP/SL)
Conditional orders triggered by Mark Price or Last Price.

V. Liquidation, Bankruptcy & ADL
Hierarchical liquidation engine utilizes partial liquidation, insurance funds, and Auto-Deleveraging (ADL).

Risk Disclaimer
Leveraged financial trading involves significant financial risks. By using MERDEX Perps, you acknowledge and agree to these terms.
