Frequently Asked Questions
What should I do if a Spot Swap fails or has high slippage? ▲
Failures are usually caused by price volatility exceeding your set limit. Please click the settings icon on the swap page and increase your Slippage Tolerance or Gas fee.
Rules for Perpetual leverage, Cross Margin, and Funding Rates ▲
Up to 100x leverage is supported. Under Cross Margin, all available USDC in your account is shared as margin. Funding rates settle every 8 hours to align index prices.
How to check/cancel open orders or fix Pending status? ▲
You can manually cancel limit orders under the Trade or Portfolio tab anytime. If a transaction is Pending due to network congestion, speed up or cancel it in your wallet.
